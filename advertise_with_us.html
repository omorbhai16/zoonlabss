Advertise With Us

Reach readers who are interested in technology, gadgets, AI tools, apps, software, and games.

About Zoon Labss

Zoon Labss is an independent technology content platform. We publish guides, explainers, comparisons, and troubleshooting articles in five areas: Product & Gadget, AI & Tools, Apps & Software, Games, and the Blog. Learn more on our About Us page.

Why Advertise With Us

  • Relevant context. Your message appears next to content about technology, software, and digital products that readers are actively researching.
  • Clear labelling. Every paid placement is marked as Sponsored or Advertisement, which keeps reader trust high for you and for us.
  • Lightweight pages. Our advertising is built to keep pages fast and readable, so your campaign does not slow the reader down.
  • Direct communication. You talk to our team by email. There are no forms, accounts, or middlemen.

We do not promise traffic, clicks, conversions, rankings, or sales. Results depend on your product, your offer, and your audience fit.

Our Audience

Our readers come to Zoon Labss to learn about products, AI tools, apps, software, and games, and to solve everyday technology problems. We do not publish estimated or promised audience numbers on this page. Verified monthly audience reports, based on screenshots from Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console, are published on the Media Kit page.

Advertising Opportunities

Banner advertising

Image banners shown on article pages.

Video advertising

Short, muted, click-to-play video units.

Sponsored articles

A clearly labelled article about your product or service.

Product and software promotion

Presenting a product, app, tool, or game to our readers.

Brand partnerships

Longer collaborations tailored to a brand.

All options are available by inquiry. Every proposal is subject to review and approval by Zoon Labss.

Banner Advertising

Banner advertisements are image units placed at the end of article pages, above the related articles. You supply the finished image and the page it should link to. We recommend:

  • A desktop image (for example 728×90 or 970×250 pixels) and, optionally, a mobile image (for example 320×100 or 300×250 pixels).
  • WebP, PNG, or JPG format, ideally under 150 KB per image.
  • Clear text, a clear brand name, and readable alternative text describing the image.

Video Advertising

Video units are shown muted and do not play automatically. Readers start playback themselves. We recommend an MP4 (H.264) or WebM file of up to 30 seconds and about 5 MB, together with a poster image. We host the file ourselves, so it loads only when the reader presses play.

Sponsored Articles

A sponsored article is written about your product, service, or topic and is clearly marked as sponsored content. Sponsored articles follow our Editorial Policy: they must be accurate, useful to readers, and free of misleading claims. We decide what is published, and we may decline or edit a proposal.

Product and Software Promotion

If you make a product, app, software, tool, or game, you can ask about promotion on Zoon Labss, for example through a banner, a video, or a sponsored article. We do not guarantee a positive review. Independent editorial articles remain independent.

Brand Partnerships

If you would like a longer collaboration, describe your idea in your email. We will tell you whether it fits our website and our readers.

How to Advertise With Us

  1. Send us an email with the information listed below.
  2. We review your inquiry and may reply with questions.
  3. If it is a fit, we agree the format, schedule, and details by email.
  4. You send the final creative files and the destination link.
  5. We review the materials and, once approved, publish the placement and mark it as sponsored.

Advertising Guidelines

  • All advertising is subject to review and approval by Zoon Labss, and we may refuse or remove any advertisement at our discretion.
  • Advertisements must be truthful and lawful and must not mislead readers about the product, price, or results.
  • We do not accept advertising for illegal products or services, malware, deceptive or fraudulent offers, hateful, violent, or sexually explicit content, or content that targets children inappropriately.
  • Destination pages must be safe, load over HTTPS, and match what the advertisement promises.
  • Paid placements are labelled Sponsored or Advertisement, and advertising links are marked as sponsored.
  • Advertising does not change our independent editorial articles.
  • We use only self-hosted images and videos. We do not run third-party advertising scripts on our pages.
