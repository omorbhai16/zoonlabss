GTA 6 After Release: Sales, Reviews, Gameplay & Latest Details

Grand Theft Auto 6 (GTA 6) has finally arrived, and its launch has quickly become one of the biggest events in video game history. Since its worldwide release on November 19, 2026, new information has emerged about its sales, reviews, gameplay, and future plans. Rockstar Games has also shared its first major launch figures. This article looks at the confirmed information reported after release, rather than relying on old rumours or pre-launch predictions.

1. GTA 6 Launch and First-Day Sales

Rockstar Games confirmed that GTA 6 became available worldwide for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026. The first major sales update arrived on November 20, when Rockstar announced that the game generated an estimated $3.79 billion in retail sales during its first 24 hours.

Rockstar also reported that 32.5 million units had been sold before the official launch. These figures show the huge demand for the game and helped it set a new entertainment launch record, according to the company.

It is important to understand that the $3.79 billion figure refers to retail sales. It should not be described as Take-Two Interactive's final net income or profit. The amount represents reported customer sales across the launch period.

2. Early Reviews and Player Response

Early reviews have been highly positive. Rockstar's parent company, Take-Two Interactive, highlighted strong responses from major gaming publications after the launch. Reviewers have praised the game's detailed environments, presentation, and the central story involving Jason and Lucia.

A Reuters report published on November 21 also described the response as broadly positive and reported a Metacritic score of 99. Review scores can change as more reviews are added, so this number should be treated as an early snapshot rather than a permanent rating.

Player reactions are still developing. Some players are focusing on the game's world and characters, while others are discussing mission design, story choices, and different parts of the experience. A balanced view will become clearer as more people spend time with the game.

3. What Players Are Noticing in Gameplay

Post-launch reviews describe a world that offers more than the usual action and driving missions. Players can spend time with the main characters during ordinary activities as well as criminal jobs. This gives the story room to show the characters' lives outside dangerous situations.

Reviews have also highlighted the way the game connects player choices with the relationship between Jason and Lucia. Their partnership is an important part of the experience, and the story gives players opportunities to see how the two characters work together.

The wider game world has received attention for its detail and variety. Reviewers have described a mix of busy city areas, outdoor locations, everyday activities, and familiar open-world action. These are observations from published reviews, not a promise that every player will experience the game in exactly the same way.

4. Jason, Lucia, and the Game World

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos are at the centre of GTA 6. The official launch information describes how a criminal job goes wrong and draws the pair into a wider conspiracy across the state of Leonida.

Their relationship is one of the main subjects discussed in early reviews. Instead of treating them only as separate action-game characters, the story places strong attention on how they depend on each other while facing difficult situations.

The game returns players to Vice City, within the fictional state of Leonida. Reviews published around launch have praised the scale and detail of this setting, along with the different activities that help make the world feel active.

5. Platforms and What Comes Next

At launch, Rockstar confirmed GTA 6 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. These are the officially announced platforms covered by the launch information.

A PC version and the future of the game's online features have become major topics after release. Reuters reported on November 20 that analysts were discussing possible future plans, including a PC launch and an online mode. However, analyst expectations are not the same as an official release announcement from Rockstar.

Players should therefore follow Rockstar's official website and news updates for confirmed platform announcements, online features, patches, and other changes.

6. Is GTA 6 Worth Playing?

Early reviews suggest that GTA 6 offers a large and detailed open-world experience, with a strong focus on its two main characters. Players who enjoy story-driven action games, exploration, driving, and open-world activities may find plenty to enjoy.

However, the game is rated M for Mature by the ESRB. Its official content information includes intense violence, strong language, nudity, sexual content, and references to drugs and alcohol. It is not suitable for every age group, so check the rating in your region before buying it.

7. Final Thoughts

GTA 6 has made a remarkable start. Its reported first-day retail sales, strong early reviews, and the attention given to its characters and world have made it a major gaming story in November 2026.

Still, the launch is only the beginning. Future updates, additional platform news, and the longer-term player response will help show how the game develops over time. For now, the most reliable approach is to follow confirmed announcements and separate official facts from predictions.
