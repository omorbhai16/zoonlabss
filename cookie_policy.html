Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Zoon Labss (zoonlabss.com) uses cookies and similar browser storage, and how you can control them. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, work in a comparable way. They help a website remember your choices and understand how it is used.

2. Tools we use

Zoon Labss uses only two Google tools to measure and manage the website (apart from the optional Translate Language menu described below): Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console. We do not use advertising, social media tracking, or any other analytics tools. We do not collect names, addresses, phone numbers, or other personal details through this website.

3. Cookies and storage items on this website

Analytics (Google Analytics 4) — only after you accept

  • _ga and _ga_<ID>: set by Google Analytics to tell visits apart and to record anonymous statistics about how pages are used. These are normally kept for up to 2 years.

These cookies are allowed only after you click Accept All Cookies (or switch on Analytics in Cookie Settings). If you choose Essential Only, analytics storage stays denied.

Essential (set by Zoon Labss)

  • zl_cookie_consent (browser local storage): remembers whether you chose "Accept All Cookies" or "Essential Only", so we do not ask you again on every page.

Language preference — only if you use Translate Language

  • googtrans (cookie) and zl_lang (local storage): remember the language you picked in the optional Translate Language menu, which uses Google's free Website Translator. They are not created unless you choose a language.

Google Search Console

Search Console is used only by the site owner to read search performance reports and to verify ownership of the site. It does not set cookies on your device when you visit Zoon Labss.

4. How to manage cookies

  • Our cookie notice: choose "Essential Only" to keep analytics storage turned off, "Accept All Cookies" to allow anonymous visit statistics, or "Cookie Settings" to pick for yourself.
  • Change your choice any time: select "Cookie Settings" in the footer of any page (under Company) to open the settings again.
  • Start over: clear cookies and site data for zoonlabss.com in your browser settings. The cookie notice will appear again on your next visit.
  • Browser settings: most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Blocking cookies will not stop you from reading our articles.
  • Google Analytics opt-out: you can also install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

5. More information

To learn how Google uses data from its services, see the Google page on how data is used when you use partner sites.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if we add or remove a tool. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest change.

7. Contact us

Questions about this Cookie Policy? Email contactwithzoonlabss@gmail.com or visit our Contact Us page.
