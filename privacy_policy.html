Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Welcome to Zoon Labss (zoonlabss.com). We publish reviews, comparisons, tutorials and guides about products and gadgets, AI tools, apps and software, and games. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you visit our website.

1. The short version

  • You do not need to create an account, sign in, or fill in any form to use Zoon Labss.
  • We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, address, phone number, or any other personal details through this website.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • The only measurement tools we use are Google Analytics 4 (anonymous visit statistics) and Google Search Console (search performance reports for site owners). We use no advertising or social-media tracking tools.

2. Information we do not collect

Zoon Labss has no registration, login, comment, newsletter, payment, or contact form. Because of this, we do not collect or store personal information such as your name, home address, phone number, or payment details. Our website does not have a user database.

3. Google Analytics 4

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors find and use our articles, so we can improve our content. GA4 provides general, aggregated statistics such as which pages are viewed, how long visits last, the type of device and browser used, the country or region of the visitor, and how visitors arrived at the site. GA4 does not log or store individual IP addresses, and we cannot identify you personally from these reports.

Analytics storage is switched off by default. It is allowed only after you choose Accept All Cookies (or switch on Analytics in Cookie Settings) in our cookie notice. If you choose Essential Only, analytics storage stays denied. For more details, see our Cookie Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Google Search Console

Google Search Console is a free tool that helps website owners see how their site appears in Google Search, for example which search terms lead to our pages and whether a page has technical problems. We use it only to verify ownership of this website and to read aggregated search reports. Search Console does not set cookies on your device when you browse our site, and we do not use it to collect personal information about visitors.

5. Language translation (optional)

The website offers an optional Translate Language button powered by Google's free Website Translator. It works only when you choose a language. In that case, the text of the page you are viewing is sent to Google to be translated, and your language choice is remembered in your browser (see our Cookie Policy). Google's handling of that data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

We use a small number of cookies and browser storage items, described in detail in our Cookie Policy. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not show third-party ads.

7. If you contact us

If you send us an email at collabzoonlabs@gmail.com or message us on Telegram or YouTube, we will receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and message. We use it only to reply to you and do not share it with anyone else. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

8. Links to other websites

Our pages may link to other websites and services, including Telegram, YouTube, and the official websites of the products, apps, and games we write about. We do not control those websites and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please read their privacy policies before sharing any information with them.

9. How we protect the website

Zoon Labss is a simple static website with no accounts and no database of visitor information, which keeps the amount of data involved very small. We take reasonable care to keep the site secure. However, no method of transmission or storage on the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's privacy

Our content is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Your choices

  • Choose Essential Only in the cookie notice to keep analytics storage turned off, and use Cookie Settings in the footer to change your choice at any time.
  • Delete cookies and site data at any time in your browser settings. The cookie notice will then appear again.
  • Install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on if you want to block Google Analytics on all websites.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features or tools. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. If we start collecting any personal information in the future, we will update this page first.

13. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email us at collabzoonlabs@gmail.com or visit our Contact Us page.
