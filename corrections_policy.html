Corrections Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Our approach

We aim to be accurate, but mistakes can happen and technology changes fast. When we find or are told about an error, we review it and correct it as soon as we reasonably can.

How to report an error

Email contactwithzoonlabss@gmail.com with the subject “Correction Request” and include:

  1. The address (URL) of the article
  2. The sentence or section that is wrong
  3. What you think is correct
  4. A source that supports it, such as an official page (if you have one)

What we do

  • We review your report and check it against reliable sources.
  • If it is correct, we fix the article. For important changes, we update the date shown on the article.
  • If we disagree, we may leave the article as it is. We may also reply to explain.
  • We cannot promise a reply time for every message.

Other issues

For a broken page, a bug, or a copyright concern, see Report an Issue or Request a Topic. Our standards are described in the Editorial Policy.
