Accessibility Statement

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to read Zoon Labss, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, or a small screen. We have not had the website formally audited or certified against an accessibility standard, so we do not claim conformance. We aim to follow good practice.

What we do

  • Semantic HTML with a clear heading structure, landmarks, and a “Skip to main content” link.
  • A menu and controls that can be used with a keyboard, with visible focus.
  • Text alternatives (alt text) for meaningful images.
  • A responsive layout that works on phones, tablets, and desktops.
  • Reduced motion respected for animated controls.
  • Readable text sizes and contrast in our design.

Known limitations

  • The language tool uses a third-party translation service, and its accessibility is outside our control.
  • Some older images or embedded material may have less helpful descriptions. We improve these as we find them.
  • Links to other websites lead to pages we do not control.

Tell us about a problem

If something on our website is hard to use, email contactwithzoonlabss@gmail.com with the page address, what you were trying to do, and the device or assistive technology you use. We will do our best to help or to fix it.
