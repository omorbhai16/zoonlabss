Editorial Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Our purpose

Zoon Labss publishes clear, practical technology content about products, AI tools, apps, software, games, and related topics. Our goal is to help readers understand something or solve a problem.

How we write

  • Plain language. We explain technical ideas simply and avoid unnecessary jargon.
  • Honesty. We describe strengths and limitations fairly and avoid exaggeration.
  • Sources. Where possible, we check facts against official websites and documentation and link to them, so readers can verify.
  • Dates. We show when an article was published and, when it changes meaningfully, when it was updated.

Use of AI tools

We may use AI tools to help with research and drafting. Content prepared with AI assistance is reviewed by our editorial team before publication, and Zoon Labss is responsible for what it publishes. AI tools do not replace checking facts.

Independence

Advertising, sponsorship, and partnerships do not decide what our independent articles say. An advertiser cannot buy a positive review or change the conclusion of an editorial article.

Sponsored content and advertising

  • Sponsored articles and advertisements are clearly labelled as Sponsored or Advertisement.
  • Links in paid placements are marked as sponsored.
  • Sponsored content must follow the same standards of accuracy and usefulness as other content, and we may decline or edit it.

Updates and corrections

We update articles when important details change. If you find an error, tell us. See our Corrections Policy.
